## About Diagrid

Diagrid (founded by the creators of CNCF-graduated **Dapr** and **KEDA**) builds developer infrastructure and APIs that simplify distributed systems and help engineering teams take **AI agents** from experimentation to production reliably, securely, and at scale.

---

## About the Role

Joining the growing **AI & DevEx** team, you will build the next generation of Diagrid's AI infrastructure and developer tools using **Node.js, TypeScript, and React**, working end-to-end across backend services, APIs, and user-facing applications.

---

## What You'll Do

- **Build end-to-end product features:** Design, develop, and maintain fullstack applications using **TypeScript, Node.js**, and modern frontend frameworks (**React**).
- **Develop scalable backend services:** Build APIs, integrations, and services powering Diagrid's AI infrastructure and developer-facing products.
- **Work on AI infrastructure:** Build tools and services enabling developers to build, deploy, manage, and operate **AI agents** reliably in production.
- **Own features concept-to-production:** Technical design, implementation, testing, deployment, and ongoing improvements.
- **Create great developer experiences (DevEx):** Intuitive interfaces, APIs, and workflows simplifying complex distributed systems.
- **Collaborate across teams:** Partner with AI, platform, product, and engineering teams across distributed time zones.
- **Champion engineering quality:** Clean, maintainable, well-tested code and architectural contributions.
- **Solve challenging technical problems:** Distributed systems, asynchronous workflows, integrations, and reliability for production AI applications.
