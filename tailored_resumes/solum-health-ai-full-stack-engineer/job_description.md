About the role

## **About Solum Health**

Solum automates the insurance work behind outpatient care: verifying benefits, getting prior authorizations approved, and finding coverage patients didn't know they had.

For an outpatient provider group, every new patient sets off the same chain of admin work. Someone checks the patient's coverage on an insurer's website, works out what's actually covered, calls the insurer when the website doesn't answer the question, pulls clinical documents together for an authorization, faxes it, and follows up for days. When any step is late or wrong, the patient's start date slips or the claim gets denied.

We replace that chain with software. Our customers include some of the largest PE-backed and outpatient provider groups in the U.S. We're backed by Y Combinator, Liquid 2 Ventures, FundersClub, and Wisdom Ventures, along with founders and operators including Immad Akhund (Mercury), Sebastian Mejia (Rappi), and Bradley Horowitz (former VP Product, Google).

## **What you'll automate**

You'll build the automations that handle insurance work for our customers, starting with outpatient facilities:

- **Insurance verification.** Confirm what a patient's plan covers and what they'll owe, quickly and accurately, without anyone waiting on hold.
- **Prior authorization.** Gather the right documents, send the request to the insurer, and follow it until there's an answer.
- **Coverage detection.** Find insurance a patient has but didn't mention, before the bill goes out.

That means AI agents that read documents, work through insurer websites, connect to the systems our customers already use, and know when to hand a case to a person. You'll write and improve the prompts behind those agents, test them on real cases, and measure how accurate they are on live work. You'll ship features across a Next.js frontend and a FastAPI backend, and use workflow tools like n8n where they're the right fit. You'll also work directly with customer teams to deploy these automations inside their organizations.

## **How we build**

At least 90% of the code we ship is written with Claude Code and Codex. Engineers here spend their time directing agents, reviewing every change carefully, writing tests, and owning what goes to production. We expect you to already work this way, and we'll ask you to show us how.
