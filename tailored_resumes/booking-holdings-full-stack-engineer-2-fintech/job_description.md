## Role Summary

Build and operate partner-facing identity verification (IDV) solutions across React micro-frontends and Java/Spring Boot backend services. Own features from technical design through production support while collaborating independently across product, design, data, platform, and vendor teams.

---

## Key Responsibilities

- Design, build, test, and operate secure, scalable end-to-end features
- Develop React/TypeScript components and micro-frontend integrations
- Build Java/Spring Boot APIs, business logic, and IDV/vendor platform integrations
- Handle authentication, workflow orchestration, retries, failures, and sensitive identity data securely
- Work with REST/GraphQL APIs, event-driven services, MySQL, caching, and Kafka
- Verify changes through unit, integration, and end-to-end tests, including standalone and host-MFE testing
- Use CI/CD and production observability — logs, metrics, traces, and alerts — to deliver and support reliable services
- Navigate ambiguity, clarify requirements, and independently drive solutions from discovery to production
- Coordinate dependencies and resolve integration or operational issues across multiple teams
- Contribute to technical design, code reviews, engineering standards, and mentoring

---

## Required Qualifications

- 6+ years of professional software engineering experience
- Strong Java and Spring Boot/Dropwizard experience, including production APIs and microservices
- Experience with React, TypeScript, HTML, CSS, and component-based frontend development
- Experience integrating third-party platforms or vendors; IDV, fraud, payments, or KYC experience is beneficial
- Knowledge of distributed systems, relational databases, efficient SQL, and automated testing
- Experience with Git, CI/CD, cloud environments, and production incident handling
- Strong ownership, communication, and ability to make pragmatic technical trade-offs
- Experience using modern AI development tools while maintaining engineering quality

---

## Nice to Have

- AWS, Kubernetes, Kafka, GraphQL and high-volume service experience
- Micro-frontend/component-service/Capla architecture and design-system experience
- Accessibility, performance tuning, experimentation, or e-commerce/travel domain knowledge
