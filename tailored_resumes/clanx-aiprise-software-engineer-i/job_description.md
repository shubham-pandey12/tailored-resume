## Job Description

Build production systems at a YC-backed AI compliance startup, owning features end-to-end across APIs, integrations, data pipelines, and risk workflows.

Software Engineer I to build and scale AiPrise's compliance platform, working across backend APIs, microservices, data pipelines, integrations, and customer-facing tools.

## Company Details

AiPrise is an AI-powered global compliance platform helping fintechs, marketplaces, and payment companies with KYC, KYB, AML, fraud prevention, and risk management.

## Requirements

- 2 years of professional software engineering experience.
- Strong programming fundamentals in Python, Go, Java, Node.js, or a similar backend language.
- Experience building REST APIs, microservices, or distributed systems.
- Strong understanding of relational databases such as PostgreSQL or MySQL.
- Familiarity with Git, CI/CD, Docker, and modern deployment practices.
- Strong debugging and problem-solving skills.
- Good communication and ability to translate business requirements into technical solutions.
- Experience with fintech, payments, fraud, compliance, AI/ML, event-driven systems, React/TypeScript, NoSQL, or infrastructure-as-code is a plus.

## Responsibilities

- Design, develop, and ship production features across backend APIs, data pipelines, integrations, and case management tools.
- Own features end-to-end from requirements and implementation through deployment and monitoring.
- Build scalable REST APIs and microservices integrating with compliance vendors.
- Develop KYB/AML case management workflows and business logic.
- Build data pipelines for ingesting, transforming, and routing compliance data.
- Integrate third-party identity and compliance APIs.
- Write clean, maintainable, tested, and documented code.
- Participate in code reviews and engineering best practices.
- Debug production issues and improve system reliability, performance, and cost efficiency.
- Build monitoring, alerting, and observability into production features.
- Collaborate with product, compliance, and customer success teams.
