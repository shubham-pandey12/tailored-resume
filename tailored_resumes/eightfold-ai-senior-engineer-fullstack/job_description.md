## About Role: Senior Full Stack Engineer (Backend-Heavy)

Our Analytics Product Engineering team is building and shipping the native reporting experience of our platform — think **conversation analytics interfaces**, **Data Hub reports**, and the **dashboards** that turn raw data into insights our customers actually use. We're looking for a full stack engineer who's happiest living on the backend but is comfortable working across the stack when needed.

### What You'll Work On
- Design and build **backend services** and **APIs** powering our **native reporting dashboards**, including handling of **row-level security (RLS)**
- Ship **full stack** features end-to-end for our **conversation analytics** interface, including handling of **row-level security (RLS)**
- Maintain the **full stack** for **Adhoc reports**
- Partner closely with **product and design** to turn **analytics requirements** into **performant, reliable** features
- Own **performance, scalability, and data correctness** for **reporting systems** handling **large volumes of analytics data**

### What We're Looking For
- Strong **backend engineering** experience (**APIs**, **services**, **data modeling**, **performance at scale**)
- Solid **full stack** chops — comfortable in the **frontend** when the feature calls for it
- Experience working with **analytics**, **reporting**, or **data-heavy products** a big plus
- Comfort working with **large datasets**, **aggregations**, and **query optimization**
- Familiarity with **row-level security (RLS)** and **data access control** patterns a plus
- A **builder mindset** — you like shipping and iterating, not just architecting on paper.
