## About Urban Company

Asia's largest tech-enabled home services marketplace — India, UAE, Singapore, Saudi Arabia. Customers book beauty/grooming, cleaning, plumbing, carpentry, appliance repair, painting and more. Deep technology stack matching millions of customers with trained service partners guaranteeing standardised, high-quality, reliable experiences.

## About the Fulfilment Team

Three core charters:
- **Planning & Shaping:** Efficiently planning supply to meet forecasted customer demand; intelligently shaping demand to meet business goals
- **Capacity Management:** Managing partner time, computing real-time availability of UC services for any given time slot, customer location, business category, and skill requirement
- **Allocation & Recovery:** Assigning the most suitable partner for each job while maintaining fairness in partner earnings; protecting customer experience in cases of no-shows, cancellations, or partner unavailability

Classic marketplace optimisation problems — forecasting, matching, scheduling, real-time recovery — at scale of millions of monthly service requests.
