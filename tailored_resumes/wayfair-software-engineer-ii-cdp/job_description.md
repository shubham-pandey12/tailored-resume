## Who We Are

Candidates for this position are preferred to be based in Bangalore, India and will be expected to comply with their team's hybrid work schedule requirements.

Wayfair helps customers find the right products for their homes. The **Customer Data Platform (CDP)** team builds and runs the systems behind Wayfair's single view of the customer — resolving customer identity across devices and channels, combining first-party and third-party data into rich customer profiles, and delivering profiles and insights at scale to power personalization, recommendations, marketing, and analytics across the company.

---

## What You'll Do

- Build and maintain backend services and high-throughput, low-latency APIs that serve customer profiles, attributes, and insights to personalization, recommendation, and marketing systems.
- Design and build well-tested batch and real-time data pipelines that collect, enrich, and maintain customer data profiles from many sources.
- Develop and improve identity resolution systems that link customer interactions across devices, sessions, and channels into a unified customer view.
- Partner with data scientists to put ML-driven customer features into production, such as embeddings, affinity and propensity scores, and segments, and make them available to downstream systems.
- Own scoped technical problems from design through implementation, testing, deployment, and ongoing maintenance.
- Make data more reliable, fresh, high-quality, and observable; investigate production and data issues and follow through on fixes.
- Help build privacy, consent, and data-governance practices into how customer data is collected, stored, and used.
- Contribute to technical designs, code reviews, documentation, and operational practices.
- Use metrics, experiments, and feedback from partner teams to improve platform quality and impact.

---

## What You'll Need

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent).
- **3–5 years** of professional software development experience.
- Strong proficiency in at least one backend programming language, such as **Java** or **Python**.
- Experience building or maintaining APIs, services, or distributed applications handling large volumes of data.
- Solid foundation in data structures, algorithms, software design, and automated testing.
- Experience with relational and non-relational databases, data modeling, and writing maintainable data-access code.
- Familiarity with production software practices: version control, CI/CD, monitoring, and debugging.
- Clear communication skills across technical and non-technical teams.

---

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience with customer data platforms, identity resolution, entity matching, or Customer 360 / master data systems.
- Experience building large-scale data pipelines with tools such as **Kafka, Spark, Dataflow, Airflow, or BigQuery**.
- Familiarity with ML-powered personalization: feature stores, customer embeddings, segmentation, or recommendation systems.
- Understanding of data privacy and consent requirements (e.g., **GDPR** or **CCPA**) and responsible PII handling.
- Experience with cloud platforms, containers, and orchestration technologies such as **GCP, Docker, or Kubernetes**.
- Experience supporting production services, including taking part in an on-call rotation.
