# Syfe - Frontend Engineer

Prepared: October 23, 2026. Source: job description supplied by the user; no external posting URL provided.

Syfe is APAC's largest and fastest-growing digital wealth platform, trusted with over US$10 billion in assets. We are fundamentally changing how hundreds of thousands of people across Asia-Pacific build wealth through a holistic approach to managing money rather than just pushing investment products. Backed by world-class investors and recognised as a leader in WealthTech, we are a team of passionate builders creating the future of wealth management.

## The Right Profile

We are looking for a talented Frontend Engineer to help build, scale, and polish the user-facing web and mobile applications that power Syfe's ecosystem. You will work closely with product managers, designers, and backend engineers to create intuitive, highly responsive, and visually stunning financial interfaces that make complex investing simple for millions of users.

## Key Responsibilities

- Design and build new features, simplify existing services, and scale our systems as we rapidly grow and evolve.
- Create reusable patterns that improve the consistency of the codebase.
- Collaborate with your engineering teammates through planning, pairing, testing, reviewing, and delivery of the features that you build.
- Work with the product team to translate powerful ideas into everyday business value.
- Write sustainable, testable, high-quality, and high-performance code.

## Minimum Qualifications

- Degree in Computer Science or other related technical field.
- 4+ years of professional software development experience.
- Strong problem-solving skills, analytical mindset, and positive attitude.
- Strong grasp of JavaScript (or TypeScript) and its nuances, including ES6+ syntax.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience with Shadcn/ui, React Query, Tailwind, Monorepos, etc.
- Experience working with ReactJS and Next.js
- Fintech Domain Knowledge is a PLUS
