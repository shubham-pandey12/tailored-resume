## Overview

Candidates for this position are preferred to be based in Bangalore, India and will be expected to comply with their team's hybrid work schedule requirements.

Wayfair is one of the world's largest destinations for the home, delivering a best-in-class customer experience in furniture and home goods. To maintain high delivery standards and meet customer needs, the Wayfair dedicated operations and logistics network focuses on speeding up deliveries, adding services, and reducing damage across physical, asset-based warehouses.

## Responsibilities

- Work with back-end engineers, UX Designers, and Product managers to understand requirements and translate design to implementation.
- Develop ready-to-ship code that is tested rigorously and follows industry best practices.
- Engage in understanding requirements and their complexities and provide well-thought-out effort estimates for tasks.
- Help in evaluating front-end technology choices for the supply chain division, introduce front-end engineering standards that other engineers can adhere to.
- Contribute to documentation and knowledge-sharing sessions for the team and the wider organization.
- Help improve software engineering practices and lead initiatives to make systems scalable, extensible, and fault tolerant.
- Come up with proof of concept solutions to help decide technology outcomes and trade-offs.
- Take complete ownership of all tasks, manage dependencies, and ensure timely completion.
- Adapt quickly to changes and devise out-of-the-box solutions when required.
