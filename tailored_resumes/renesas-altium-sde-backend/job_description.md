## Job Description

**Part Analytics Mission:** Transforming Sourcing into a Competitive Advantage. Our AI-powered supply management software empowers manufacturing companies to accelerate product development, improve profit margin and uncover hidden risks in the supply chain.

### About the Role

As part of this role, you will be doing development, write unit test case, high level test case and deliver quality application.

- Accurate estimation and timely completion of tasks.
