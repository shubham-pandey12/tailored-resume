## About Headout

$200M+ in revenue, guests in 700+ cities, 18 months of profitability — Headout is the fastest-growing marketplace in the travel industry. The platform lets users discover and book real-life experiences: tours, museums, live events, and more. Raised $60M+ from top-tier investors; profitable and growing.

## Role Overview

As a Frontend Engineer at Headout, you'll be part of a pod building products and features that directly impact users. You'll design and develop high-performance web applications, work closely with product and design teams, and ensure smooth operations. Emphasis on end-to-end ownership, innovation, and working in a fast-paced startup environment.

---

## What Makes This Role Special

- Build projects end-to-end, from scratch to launch
- Take ownership of features and bring innovative solutions to life
- Collaborate with experienced developers on diverse projects
- Ideas are valued; contributions directly shape the product
- Fast-paced startup environment with real-world scale
