## About Motion

Motion is a North American transportation solutions company helping fleets save money, move smarter, and operate more efficiently. Partners with carriers of all sizes to provide discounted fuel programs, flexible financial services, and digital tools for fleet operations. Mission: remove friction for trucking companies managing costs, back-office processes, and growth.
