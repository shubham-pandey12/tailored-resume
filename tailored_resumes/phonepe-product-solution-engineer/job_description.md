## Company

PhonePe Limited (Formerly PhonePe Private Limited) is a technology company that builds digital platforms for Payments, Digital Distribution Services and Financial Services. Headquartered in India, the PhonePe digital payments app was launched in 2016. As of April 2026, PhonePe has over 70 Crore life-till-date registered users and a digital payments acceptance network spread across over 5 Crore merchants. PhonePe's products and services include Consumer Payments (including Digital Distribution Services), Merchant Payments, Lending and Insurance Distribution services, and New Platforms, which comprise Share.Market (stock broking and mutual funds distribution platform), and Indus Appstore (Android-based mobile app marketplace).

## Role Summary

Seeking a motivated and skilled Product Solution Engineer with 3+ years of experience. The ideal candidate will have a strong foundation in production support, troubleshooting, and automation. This role requires a proactive problem-solver who can maintain system stability, automate manual tasks, and effectively bridge the gap between technical operations and business requirements.

## Key Responsibilities

- Diagnose, troubleshoot, and fix production software issues efficiently
- Develop and own monitoring solutions for both production and non-production environments and applications

- Provide valuable feedback from customer users to the internal engineering and product teams
