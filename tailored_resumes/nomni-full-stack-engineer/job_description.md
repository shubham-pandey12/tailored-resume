## About Nomni

Nomni builds an all-in-one hospitality operations platform—bringing POS, payments, ordering, loyalty, procurement, marketing, and data together with AI at its core—serving 35,000+ venues across Australia and Southeast Asia.

---

## About the Role

This role is **frontend-led** while requiring technical breadth across mobile applications, backend services, integrations, databases, and supporting infrastructure. You'll join a small, fast-moving team owning features from early concept and prototyping through testing, deployment, and production support.

---

## What You'll Do

- Build polished product experiences across **web and mobile**.
- Work primarily with **React, React Native, Next.js, TypeScript, and JavaScript**.
- Develop supporting services and APIs using **Node.js**.
- Work with **Supabase, databases, REST APIs, and third-party integrations**.
- Take features from early concepts through implementation, testing, deployment, and production.
- Make practical decisions regarding architecture, tooling, and technical trade-offs.
- Bring structure and clarity to rapidly evolving products and incomplete requirements.
- Use modern **AI-assisted development workflows** to improve delivery speed and productivity.
- Build, integrate, and improve **AI-enabled product capabilities**.
- Support **AI evaluation, experimentation, observability, tracing, and debugging**.
- Maintain and improve products following launch; manage multiple projects with end-to-end ownership.

- Strong track record building and shipping real products.
- Excellent frontend engineering capabilities with a strong eye for usability and product quality.
