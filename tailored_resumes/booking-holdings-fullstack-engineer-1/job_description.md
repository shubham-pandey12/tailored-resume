## About the Company

Booking Holdings is the world's leading provider of online travel & related services, provided to consumers and local partners in more than 220 countries and territories through six primary consumer-facing brands: Booking.com, Priceline, Agoda, Rentalcars.com, KAYAK and OpenTable.

Booking Holdings Bangalore is a Center of Excellence supporting the increasing business demands of the Booking Holdings Brands, providing access to specialized and highly skilled talent, leading industry best practices, and collaboration opportunities across all Booking Holdings brands.

## About the Role

As a Fullstack developer, you will be developing and optimizing applications, ensuring they are scalable, flexible and high performing. Responsible for your work across the tech stack. **Backend heavy; frontend working knowledge is a plus.**

---

## Primary Responsibilities

- Implementation of technical solutions based on business requirements
- Estimation of effort and impact of items you work on
- Show high quality of craft/work in what you deliver
- Quality of automated tests is equally important as production code; refactor both when needed
- Collaborate with colleagues in other job roles and teams to design, prioritise and implement technical tasks
- Deliverables can range from product features to infrastructure automation, frameworks, tooling or services
- Partner with Senior Engineers and peers to build new capabilities and reuse existing capabilities
- Own the design and delivery of software solutions, deploying and monitoring code in production with some supervision

---

## Role Qualifications and Requirements

- Minimum 3 years of experience in full stack web application development
- Proven expertise with server-side programming languages (Node.js, Java) — **preferably Node.js**
- Strong proficiency in modern front-end technologies (JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Vue.js, or Angular) — **preferably React**
- Extensive experience with relational databases (MySQL, PostgreSQL) and familiarity with NoSQL databases
- Prior experience with implementing microservices and microfrontends
- Experience working on **horizontal platform products** is a significant advantage
- Docker, Kubernetes (plus)
- AWS (preferred)
