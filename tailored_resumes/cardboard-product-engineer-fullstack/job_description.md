## Engineering

Cardboard is a video editor you can talk to. Describe the video you want, and our agent builds the edit on a real timeline. It runs in the browser and as a Mac app.

We're hiring a Product Engineer (Fullstack) to own features end to end, then watch people use them and decide what's next.

## What you'll own

- Product features across the stack, from the editor to the services behind it.
- Getting more people from an idea to a finished video.
- A fast, reliable editor, even on big projects.

## Who you are

- You've shipped product people use, and made the product calls along the way.
- You're strong across the stack and notice when something feels slow or janky.
- You'd rather ship something small today than plan something big for a month.
- Bonus: creative tools, video, or real-time editors.

## How we work

- You own outcomes, not tickets: decide what to build, ship it, and check it worked.
- You talk to users and look at the data.
- You build with AI agents every day, and you're the taste and judgment in the loop.

## What success looks like

Within your first six months:

- You own an area of the product, and the team comes to you for decisions about it.
- You've shipped things users rely on every week, and can show how they changed usage.
