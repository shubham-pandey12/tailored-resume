## Likely Screening Questions

**1. Tell me about a project you built without being asked.**

WebHealth — I noticed that website auditing required switching between several tools manually (Lighthouse, security scanners, SEO checkers) and built a unified platform from scratch. Self-initiated, end-to-end: Python backend, React frontend, custom multi-page web crawler, Google Lighthouse integration, passive security scanning, and automated background reporting deployed on GCP. No one asked me to build it; I spotted the gap and shipped it.

**2. Describe a time you owned a project from requirements through production.**

At Moresand Technologies, I owned the design and delivery of a distributed multi-tenant CMS platform end-to-end — from schema design and API contracts through CI/CD pipeline setup, production deployment, and ongoing incident response. Clarified requirements with stakeholders, broke work into phases, and drove delivery without close supervision.

**3. How do you use AI tools in your development workflow?**

I use GitHub Copilot for code completion and Claude for design discussions, debugging, and documentation. At MAQ Software, I applied Claude throughout the development loop for an AI assistant backend — including generating test scaffolding and validating AI output against production requirements before shipping. I treat AI-generated output as a starting point that needs review, not a final answer.

**4. How do you handle a production incident?**

At Moresand, I was responsible for production incident response: monitoring health metrics and alerts, triaging issues, identifying root cause, and deploying fixes with zero downtime. I maintained runbooks for known failure modes and authored post-incident write-ups to close the loop with stakeholders.

**5. Why Abnormal AI?**

Abnormal is building AI-native security products at significant scale — a technically interesting problem with real customer impact. The MSP team's early-stage charter means real ownership of what gets built, alongside experienced engineers. That combination of ownership and senior mentorship is exactly what I am looking for at this stage.

**6. What is your experience with background jobs and scheduled pipelines?**

At Moresand, I designed and built scheduled background jobs and data collection pipelines as part of the multi-tenant platform — owning the scheduling logic, failure handling, and monitoring. The pipeline aggregated brand-specific data across internal systems on a recurring basis.
