## About Harness

Harness is the AI Software Delivery Platform company, led by technologist and entrepreneur Jyoti Bansal (founder of AppDynamics, acquired by Cisco for $3.7B). Harness brings AI and automation to the "outer loop" of software delivery — testing, deployments, application security, reliability, compliance, and cost optimization — powered by Harness AI and the Software Delivery Knowledge Graph.

## About the Role

Are you a software engineer who loves deconstructing complex problems and enjoys applying your skills to enable customer success? Join our **Customer Engineering** team in **Bangalore** in this unique hybrid role.

This position blends deep technical software engineering expertise with a customer-facing focus. You will serve as the technical authority for enterprise customers, leveraging expertise in **CI/CD** and **DevOps** to architect solutions, troubleshoot complex technical challenges, and optimize software delivery pipelines across **CI/CD**, **Feature Flags**, **Cloud Cost Management**, and **Security Testing Orchestration (STO)**.

### Key Responsibilities
- Act as the trusted technical expert and primary point of contact for customers, using software engineering expertise to guide architecture and implementation of sophisticated **CI/CD** strategies and the entire **SDLC**.
- **Diagnose and resolve complex technical issues:** Dive deep into customer environments, code, and configurations to troubleshoot pipeline execution, infrastructure integration, and performance.
- **Master the Harness Platform:** Develop code-level expertise across Harness modules (**CI**, **CD**, **Feature Flags**, **Cloud Cost**, and **STO**).
- Lead end-to-end architecture and implementation of scalable, secure, high-performance CI/CD platforms across **multi-cloud** and **hybrid** ecosystems.
- Drive large-scale **migrations** and **standardization**, optimizing pipelines, enforcing **governance**, and influencing product direction.
- Translate customer pain points and requirements into best-practice solutions integrating Harness modules.
- Collaborate with **Product Management and Engineering** via detailed use cases, bug fixes, and code contributions.
- Develop and deliver advanced **technical workshops** and **run books** to empower customers.

## Qualifications & Must-Have Skills

- **2–6 years** of experience in a software engineering role focused on **DevOps**, **CI/CD**, and **automation**, with passion for a technical **customer-facing** role.
- Proven passion for **deep-dive troubleshooting** — analyzing **code**, **logs**, and **configurations** to find **root cause** and solutions.
- Strong programming and scripting skills: proficiency in one or more of **Go, Python, Java, or Shell**.
- Hands-on experience with CI/CD platforms (**Harness**, **Jenkins**, **GitLab CI**, **GitHub Actions**, or **CircleCI**).
- Solid understanding of containerization (**Docker**) and orchestration (**Kubernetes**).
- Experience with Infrastructure as Code (**IaC**) tools such as **Terraform** or **Ansible**.
- Familiarity with cloud platforms (**AWS**, **GCP**, or **Azure**).
- Excellent problem-solving skills and strong sense of ownership.
