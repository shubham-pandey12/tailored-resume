## Role Overview

Senior individual contributor owning backend services for a large-scale enterprise SaaS platform (Kanban-style work management and flow). Designs and ships features end-to-end, collaborating closely with frontend, product, and QA.

---

## What You'll Do

- Design, implement, and maintain **Node.js (TypeScript/JavaScript)** backend services and APIs
- Model and evolve core domain entities (boards, lanes, work items, WIP rules, permissions, cross-team connections)
- Build secure, **multi-tenant REST/JSON APIs** consumed by web frontends and internal services
- Implement business logic for work-flow, metrics, and cross-team visibility
- Improve **performance, reliability, and observability** of existing services
- Integrate backend services with data storage and analytics layers
- Write and maintain automated tests (unit, integration, contract)
- Partner with product to refine requirements and break work into incremental, shippable slices
