## About Navan

Navan is the all-in-one travel and expense management platform that makes it easy for companies to manage travel, expenses, and corporate cards at scale. Navan serves thousands of companies globally, from startups to Fortune 500 enterprises.

---

## Role Overview

As a Senior Fullstack Engineer at Navan, you will build and maintain scalable, enterprise-grade software that powers business travel and expense management for thousands of companies worldwide. You will work across the full stack — Java/Spring Boot backend, React/TypeScript frontend — and own features end-to-end from design through production.

---

## Key Responsibilities

- Design, develop, and maintain robust, scalable fullstack features across Java/Spring Boot backend and React/TypeScript frontend
- Own data modelling and query optimisation for complex enterprise schemas (MySQL, PostgreSQL)
- Build and maintain configurable approval workflows, policy-based configuration tools, and role/permission management systems
- Drive microservice architecture decisions, API design, and service reliability at enterprise scale
- Maintain production observability — structured logging, metrics, alerting, and incident response
- Mentor junior engineers and champion engineering best practices across the team
- Collaborate with product and design to ship high-quality features with measurable business impact

---

## Required Qualifications

- 4–6 years of professional software engineering experience
- Strong Java and Spring Boot experience (primary backend stack)
- React + TypeScript proficiency for frontend development
- Experience with relational databases: MySQL, PostgreSQL — data modelling, query optimisation, transactions
- Microservices architecture — designing service boundaries, REST APIs, service reliability
- AWS cloud experience (EC2, S3, ECS or similar)
- Docker and Kubernetes for containerised deployments
- Approval workflows, policy engines, or role/permission management experience — a plus

---

## Nice-to-Have

- Experience with Angular.js or other frontend frameworks
- AI dev tools (GitHub Copilot, Claude, Cursor) in daily workflow
- System design experience: complex joins, indexing strategies, transactional integrity
- CI/CD pipeline ownership (Jenkins, Docker, AWS)
