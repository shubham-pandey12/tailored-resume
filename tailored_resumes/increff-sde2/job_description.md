## About Increff

Increff is the most preferred retail SaaS partner, solving complex inventory management and supply chain challenges for retailers. Core focus: merchandising and omni-channel inventory management solutions for brands and retailers.

**Vision:** To be the most admired retail technology company.

---

## About the Role

End-to-end ownership of a technical area — plan it, design it, ship it, run it in production, hold its quality bar, and grow the engineers working on it. Write and review a lot of code; accountable for whether the team delivers predictably.

---

## What You'll Own

| Area | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Planning** | Turn product goals into technical milestones. Write HLDs/LLDs. Surface dependencies and risks early. Track progress and correct course when delivery drifts. |
| **Platform** | Build and scale reliable, high-throughput systems that power Increff's products and enable seamless business operations. |
| **Delivery** | Run stand-ups and sprint planning, make commitments the team can meet, own release readiness: scope, testing, risks, cut-offs. |
| **Solutioning** | Design and build major and high-risk components. Drive refactoring where it pays off. Balance MVP scope against maintainability. |
| **Quality** | Review for correctness, security, performance, and reliability. Act as merge-quality gatekeeper. Keep test coverage sufficient. RCA on what slips through. |
| **Mentorship** | Coach 3–4 SDE-I engineers through reviews, pairing, and design walkthroughs until they own features end to end. |
| **Production health** | Own alerts, dashboards, runbooks, and incident response. Protect data correctness and security. Keep docs current. |
| **Productivity** | Automate repetitive work and quality checks. Use AI for research, task breakdown, and edge-case discovery — and validate its output. |
