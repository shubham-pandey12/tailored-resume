## The Role

Design the system a household bets its day on. M is agentic software in production, and agentic systems are unforgiving: a loop that doesn't converge burns money, a weak boundary corrupts a tenant's data, a slow write makes a household wait. As we scale, you'll take over architecture ownership and set the foundations: the boundaries, the safety, the scale, the standards.

## Requirements

- 4 to 6 years in backend and distributed systems, including real architecture ownership.
- You've designed systems that stayed correct and affordable under scale and concurrency.
- Deep judgment on data modelling, consistency, isolation, and failure modes.
- You've made a fast-growing team better through standards, review, and mentorship.
- Calm with ambiguity; you bring order to it.

## Bonus Qualifications

- Worked on agentic/AI systems, event-sourced architectures, or high-stakes transactional systems.
- Tamed a runaway cost or reliability problem in a complex system.
- Opinions on where automated systems need hard limits and human confirmation.

## Responsibilities

- Own backend architecture as the team and product scale several-fold.
- Design the boundaries and safety that keep autonomous, AI-driven systems correct, bounded, and affordable.
- Get multi-tenant isolation, reliability, and performance right.
- Make the hard design and build-vs-buy calls, and write the standards the team builds against.
- Raise the bar through review and mentorship.
