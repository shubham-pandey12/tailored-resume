## About Crunchyroll

Founded by fans, Crunchyroll delivers the art and culture of anime to a passionate community. We super-serve over 100 million anime and manga fans across 200+ countries and territories, and help them connect with the stories and characters they crave. Whether that experience is online or in-person, streaming video, theatrical, games, merchandise, events and more, it's powered by the anime content we all love.

## About the Role

As a Software Engineer on the Service Monetization Team, you will help shape the future of our Service Monetization Platform. You will architect, build and optimize our subscriptions and payments platform that enables membership and powers revenue collection for Crunchyroll. Working with design, product, quality and data teams, you will solve complex challenges, optimize performance, and enhance user experience, directly contributing to the growth of the Crunchyroll fanbase.

## About You

- Experienced and passionate Software Engineer with experience building robust and reliable backend services (2+ years of relevant experience)
- Experienced in Java, GoLang, Python, PHP, Typescript or Node.js
- Experienced with cloud technologies (API Gateway, Lambda, EC2, RDS, ECS)
- Experienced with databases (PostgresDB/MariaDB, DynamoDB/MongoDB)
- Experienced in operational excellence (on-call rotations, incident management)
- Excel in an Agile and collaborative environment

## About the Team

The Service Monetization organization is responsible for building, maintaining, and securing systems that enable membership and collect payments information for Crunchyroll users. We are directly tied to many streams of revenue and pride ourselves on quality code, service reliability, and teamwork. Day to day work includes integrations with payment providers, payments fraud detection and prevention, optimizations to reduce passive churn while maintaining PCI compliance. Services process millions of payments in a highly secure, distributed, and scalable environment.
